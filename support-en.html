FAQ

How do I play? Put your finger on the screen and drag. Weapons fire automatically. The round button at the bottom right triggers your ultimate.

Do I need internet? No, Last Exit runs fully offline.

My progress is gone. Your save game lives only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Are there purchases? Version 1.0 has no real-money purchases.
