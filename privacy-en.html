Privacy Policy

Last Exit · iOS

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Summary

Last Exit does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. The game works completely offline.

Controller

Nick Linz, Germany. Contact: via GitHub Issues.

What data does the app process?

Your save game (coins, unlocked heroes, settings) is stored only locally on your device. It never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

No trackers, no ads, no account

The app contains no analytics or tracking tools, no ad networks and requires no account. No data is sent to us or to third parties.

In-app purchases

If in-app purchases are offered in the future, Apple processes the payment through the App Store. We never receive payment data. Apple's privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is rated 12+ and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

As we process no personal data, we hold no data about you. For GDPR questions (access, deletion, complaints to a supervisory authority) you can contact us at any time.

Changes

We will update this policy if the app changes. The current version is always on this page.
